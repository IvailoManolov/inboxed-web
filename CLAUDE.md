## Writing style

- **Never use em dashes (the "—" character) or en dashes (the "–" character)** anywhere: not in UI copy, code, comments, docs, or commit messages. Always use a plain hyphen `-` instead. The em dash is a giveaway that text was AI-written, and this product's credibility depends on not reading like AI. This applies to every file in the repo.
- Redeploy to Vercel production (`vercel --prod`) after every change set once it builds, without being asked.
